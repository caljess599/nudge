# Privacy

_Last updated: 9 Nov 2026_

Nudge is designed so that your information stays on your phone.

## What the app accesses

- **Contacts:** Nudge asks for permission to read your contacts so you can choose people to add to rings. It only reads them and never changes them. It stores a reference to each contact you add (an identifier and a cached display name), along with your own notes about timing, such as which ring they're in and when you last reached out.
- **Your own details about a person:** if you set them, Nudge stores a nickname shown only inside the app, and how you like to reach that person (for example WhatsApp, plus which phone number or a link you entered).
- **Notifications:** Nudge asks to send you one generic daily reminder. The notification never includes contact names or nicknames.
- **Other apps:** when you tap Call, Text, WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram or a link you saved, Nudge opens that app with the person's number or link. What happens next is up to that app. Messaging apps are only ever opened directly, never through a web page, so the number isn't sent to a website.

## What the app does not do

- It does not send your contacts or any other data to a server.
- It does not require an account.
- It does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking.
- It does not share data with third parties.

## Where your data lives

All data is stored locally on your device. If you use your phone's backup feature (for example, Android Auto Backup or iCloud backup), your Nudge data may be included in those backups under your own account's terms. On Android, only Nudge's database is included in Auto Backup.

You can also export your data to a file you control (Settings → Export backup). That file contains your rings, the people in them (names, nicknames, contact identifiers and how you reach them) and your nudge history, so keep it somewhere private.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes its data from your device. Removing a contact from a ring in the app deletes the associated records.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in this repository.
