# Privacy

_Last updated: 8 Nov 2026

Nudge is designed so that your information stays on your phone.

## What the app accesses

- **Contacts:** Nudge asks for permission to read your contacts so you can choose people to add to rings. It stores a reference to each contact you add (an identifier and a cached display name), along with your own notes about timing, such as which ring they're in and when you last reached out.
- **Notifications:** Nudge asks to send you one generic daily reminder. The notification never includes contact names.

## What the app does not do

- It does not send your contacts or any other data to a server.
- It does not require an account.
- It does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking.
- It does not share data with third parties.

## Where your data lives

All data is stored locally on your device. If you use your phone's backup feature (for example, Android Auto Backup or iCloud backup), your Nudge data may be included in those backups under your own account's terms. You can also export your data to a file you control.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes its data from your device. Removing a contact from a ring in the app deletes the associated records.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in this repository.
